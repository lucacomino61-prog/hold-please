# Albanian meme characters and TikTok creators — public personas

> **As callers in the game:** `tools/callers/{memes,creators,figures}.mjs` turn these personas into Character Creator
> callers; `node tools/callers/build.mjs` writes `public/packs/albanian-callers.json` (166 callers) plus one file per
> part. Load them with Harbour OS → Character Creator → Import JSON (the file is also served at
> `/packs/albanian-callers.json` by the dev server). The live brain (brain/ proxy) plays them from `gimmick`, `world`,
> `quirks` and `question`. Left out on purpose: the undocumented handles, the religious figures, the deceased, and any
> trait tied to a criminal case or a tragedy. Real names are used because that was the brief; see the caution in
> the session notes before shipping.

Research notes, 19 October 2026. Every line describes the **public image** a person projects in press
coverage, show clips, rankings or their own posts; nothing here is a claim about who they are in private.
Where the open web shows only a handle and a follower count (TikTok itself blocks crawlers), the entry says
"undocumented" instead of inventing a character. Follower counts are third-party estimates (hafi.pro,
19 Oct 2026; Favikon, 2026; Heepsy). Pages, brands, fan accounts and media outlets are left out.

Legend: **Image** = the persona as it appears in public. **Voice** = how they talk / what they post.
**Basis** = what the persona rests on (press, clips, rankings).

## Part 1 — Meme characters (the people who became memes)

### The "Ujku" family and their circle

**Ujku i Shijakut** (the Wolf of Shijak) · Shijak · real name never published
- Origin: Top Channel's *Shqipëria Live*, May 2020, an episode on people who got famous on social media.
- Image: self-made legend. Claims he slept in forests and tunnels as a boy and hid six months in the woods
  living on raw "bio" meat, hence the name. A tracksuit tough guy with a soft centre: bashful in interviews,
  boastful on camera, dances tallava in the GIFs.
- Voice: short boasts and survival stories; says little, repeats the legend. His one confession is the kiss
  with Kiçja on *D-Night* (Nov 2021): "my mistake, I don't forgive myself".
- Afterlife: TikTok sounds and "gjyshi" edits, a Spotify single "Ujku Shijakut (Meme 1)", Tenor GIFs.
- Basis: Top Channel, Boldnews, Oranews, Tenor, Spotify, TikTok tag pages.

**Kiçja** (Kristjana Ndreçka) · Instagram personality
- Image: glam diva of the Shijak/Durrës circuit. Flaunts money (claims 50,000 euro a month), sings on
  TV (*Why Not*, TV Vora, "Kjo hardhi e rrushit"), says she would act in an adult film.
- Voice: provocative, confident, celebrity-adjacent, never embarrassed. In memes she is the unattainable
  woman the Wolf kissed once and regretted.
- Basis: TV Vora clips, Boldnews.

**Ujku i Fierit** (the Wolf of Fier) · Fier
- Origin: interview clips with Ledjan Muçaraku in the *Zonë e Lirë* orbit, around 2020–21.
- Image: old-school hard man who will not be interrupted. Military self-talk ("don't call me general, I am
  the wolf of…"), folksy insults ("mos bëj si këmbët e dhisë" / "don't act like goat legs"), a life of
  suffering and no fear of the police. Loyal to the other wolves (defends Ujku i Cërrikut), feuds with a
  certain Ardi Daullxhiu.
- Voice: gravelly monologues, threats wrapped in proverbs, demands respect.
- Basis: YouTube clip titles and descriptions, Instagram ujkufierit_official.

**Ledjan "Ledion" Muçaraku** · Fier
- Image: the "supermashtrues" (super-fraudster) turned showman. Press and clip titles describe a convicted
  con man who now does provocative interviews and feuds: insults Arian Çani on air, sings a song for Sali
  Berisha, gets profiled as "fraudster, madman, or both".
- Voice: fast, theatrical, self-narrating; the con man who tells you how the con works and dares you to
  mind.
- Basis: YouTube (Zonë e Lirë channel and others), A1 Report.

**Ujku i Cërrikut** (the Wolf of Cërrik) · Cërrik
- Image: the third wolf, known only through others: Ujku i Fierit defends him, a Zëri crime headline mixes
  him up with the Shijak one. Persona undocumented.

**Ujku i Kavajës**, **Ujku i Bajzës** · exist only as a Tenor tag and a TikTok tag page. Undocumented.

### TikTok-live and comedy personas

**Kozaku** (Kozak Braçi, @kozak.megalluks69, ~558K) · Tirana area
- Image: the TikTok-live brawler. His whole act is confrontation: profanity-first tirades at viewers and
  rivals ("fuck you, fuck your mother" is the register), feuds with other streamers and with convicted
  criminals. Press: seized and beaten on a livestream by a convict's brother after insulting him on
  Instagram; explosives placed at a building he rented.
- Voice: shouting, swearing, dares; no jokes, only aggression as entertainment.
- Basis: Shqiptarja, Panorama, hafi.pro, ViralShqipLIVE channel.

**Dojoni** · Kozaku's live-stream counterpart on the compilation channels. Undocumented beyond that.

**Eni Kapxhiu** · impressions
